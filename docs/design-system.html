Foundations

paper · ink · brand

The palette is cool and quiet so the brand blue and status colors carry meaning. Cards sit on --surface white above a --paper canvas; structure comes from borders, not shadows. The clinical blue is reserved for primary actions, the active state, and focus rings.

Spacing · radius · elevation · motion

borders over shadows

Cards use a 12px radius; buttons, chips and badges are full pills; inputs and icon buttons use 10px. Elevation is reserved — only the app frame, toasts, and floating controls get --shadow-frame. Everything else is defined by hairline borders.
