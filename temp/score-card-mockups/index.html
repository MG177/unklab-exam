Baseline — current ScoreCard

Nested lavender-tint wrapper, white 400×200 inner card, thick red accent ring, separate shadow pills for name and correct count.

Pros

  • Distinct visual identity students may already recognize
  • Score and name are visually separated
  • Works in admin exam detail too (reused component)

Cons

  • Triple nesting + md:scale-125 fights responsive layout
  • Red ring implies danger, not achievement
  • Shadows everywhere vs design-system “borders over shadows”
  • Scores not in mono / tabular nums
